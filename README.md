## About Me

Mohammad Pedrami is a Human Resource Management researcher and practitioner with expertise in Compensation and Benefits, HR Analytics, and emerging HR technologies. His research focuses on Artificial Intelligence adoption in Human Resource Management, Human–AI Teaming, behavioral nudges, ethical implications of AI, and evidence-based organizational practices through meta-synthesis and systematic review methodologies.

His work aims to bridge academic research and professional HR practice by leveraging data-driven methodologies and innovative frameworks to enhance organizational effectiveness, support strategic decision-making, and promote ethical and responsible applications of Artificial Intelligence in workplace environments.
