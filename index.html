Mohammad Pedrami

Human Resource Management researcher and practitioner working across Compensation & Benefits, HR Analytics, and the study of Artificial Intelligence in the workplace.

His research examines AI adoption in Human Resource Management, Human–AI Teaming, behavioral nudges, and the ethical implications of workplace AI, built through meta-synthesis and systematic review methodology. The aim throughout: connect academic research with day-to-day HR practice, using evidence and data to support better organizational decisions.
